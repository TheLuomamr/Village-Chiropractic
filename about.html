About Dr. Luoma

Personal, unhurried care from a chiropractor who takes the time to know you.

Dr. Nathan Luoma earned his Bachelor of Arts degree in English from The College of Wooster before pursuing his Doctor of Chiropractic degree at University in Marietta, Georgia.

Dedicated to providing the highest quality care, Dr. Luoma continually expands his knowledge through advanced continuing education, professional seminars, and ongoing review of the latest chiropractic research. His approach emphasizes individualized treatment plans, recognizing that every patient and condition requires a unique strategy. By combining evidence-based care with a genuine passion for helping others, Dr. Luoma strives to help patients achieve lasting health and wellness.

His commitment to chiropractic excellence and compassionate patient care led him to partner with Dr. Brett Taylor in opening One Life Chiropractic, where he has helped countless individuals improve their quality of life through natural, patient-focused treatment.

Recently, Dr. Luoma and his wife relocated to Birmingham, Alabama, to be closer to family and begin an exciting new chapter with their two children. He looks forward to continuing his mission of serving others, building meaningful relationships, and making a positive impact in his new community through exceptional chiropractic care.
